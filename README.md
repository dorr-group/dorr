# DORR Hospitality Group — website

Three versions of the site, each self-contained (fonts, scripts and images are all in this repository; no third-party requests) and installable on a phone via "Add to Home Screen".

- Version 1: `/` (root)
- Version 2: `/v2/`
- Version 3: `/v3/`

Published with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.

## Version 5 (`v5/`)
"The Dive", inspired by one reference only (ERA Residence). Open `v5/index.html`.
- The O of the DORR wordmark is a live iridescent pearl that opens into a window you fall through
- The opening horizon follows the visitor's local time (Daylight / Moonlight), with a manual switch
- The structure drawn on scroll as a necklace: the Holding is the clasp, the eight departments are beads, the five brands are pearls
- Brands one pearl at a time in oval frames; the page ends in the deep with the contact form

## Version 6 (`v6/`)
"Nacre", inspired by one reference only (Son Daven). Open `v6/index.html`.
- The hero photo (mountains at low sun) is drawn as rippling growth rings, then opens like a pearl
- Dusk palette of sand on deep umber, sand cards over full-bleed photography
- The structure grows ring by ring: Holding, eight departments, five brands
- Brands on a sideways strip of pill-shaped frames; contact in an arched card over embers


## Version 8 (`v8/`)
"Strand": one thread, many pearls. Open `v8/index.html`.
- The Holding Company is the clasp, the Services Company the thread, the five brands the pearls
- Photos redrawn live as rows of shaded pearls on sagging threads (WebGL) that resolve into the photograph
- A necklace that strings itself on scroll; brands beside one round viewer that re-strings for each

## Version 7 (`v7/`)
"The shell opens": an editorial pearl-and-shell site. Open `v7/index.html`.
- Loader where a pearl forms its growth rings and the screen parts along a scalloped seam
- Hero photo in a pearl oval, the structure as nacre layers (Holding at the centre, eight departments, five brands)
- Scalloped shell cards, a brand index with an oval preview, Future Vision in a growing pearl, a separate contact page

## Version 4 (`v4/`)
"The pearl is DORR": one continuous 3D scroll film (three.js, self-hosted). Open `v4/index.html`.
- One crafted shell resting on still water, light glowing through its joints; scrolling opens it on its hinge
- The DORR pearl inside: tap it for the group introduction and structure
- Five brand pearls built from points and lines, each a different construction; tap one for its full sheet
- The eight disciplines as beads on a thread of light, Future Vision as an orrery, then the readable dossier

## Version 9 (`v9/`)
"A day, held still", in the spirit of Aman's calm. Open `v9/index.html`.
- Full-bleed, slow brand film cut from free-licence stock footage (credits in the page footer), with optional ambient sound that starts off
- The page moves from dawn linen to dusk and an ink night band; centred wordmark, quiet serif typography
- Structure, eight disciplines, five brand rows, Future Vision and contact

## Version 10 (`v10/`)
A refined copy of the approved Version 4. Open `v10/index.html`.
- One gesture, one step: the film moves chapter by chapter; the menu and rail jump straight to a chapter
- Tap a pearl to fly inside its points and lines and see the brand (or the group) there; Back flies out again
- Softer, slower motion and new sound effects tuned to the music's key
